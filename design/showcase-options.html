Your showcase media is 1934×1072 rendered at 699px wide. The scale factor, 36%, is the one number here I am certain of.

A correction, before you look. I first told you this made 14px UI text land at 5.1px, which would be invisible. That assumed the recording was captured at 1x. It almost certainly was not: a 1934px-wide frame of a browser window is a retina capture, so the true figure is roughly double and the text lands somewhere around 8 to 11px. Small and uncomfortable, not unreadable. I overstated it, and the honest version is weaker than the one I gave you.
